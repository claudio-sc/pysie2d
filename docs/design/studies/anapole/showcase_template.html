Limits

  • The deepest designs are needle-like, with curvature radii of a few nm. No fabrication rule was imposed.
  • Deep dips are broadband zeros: csca grows as (Δλ)², and widths at ×10 and ×100 differ by about √10. Req = 700 nm instead sits on a 0.2 nm Fano feature.
  • Above about 360 nm usually one start in eight finds the best value, so darker designs likely exist.
  • Fixed index and lossless silicon. Converged to ≤ 3·10⁻⁵ in boundary points; the circle matches Mie to 10⁻¹⁵.
